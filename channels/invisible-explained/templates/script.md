# Script template

Fill in, then put the finished text in `script.voiceText`.

```
[<delivery tag: e.g. deep, cinematic documentary narrator, calm authority, steady pace>] <HOOK: question or instruction, ≤ 8 words, felt in 2 s>
<SETUP: 1 sentence that makes the mystery concrete>
[leaning in] <MECHANISM: 2–3 short sentences, the invisible process, one CAPITALISED stress word>
[building] <EVIDENCE or STAKES: one surprising, sourced fact or experiment>
[pause] <PAYOFF: one line that reframes the idea>
```

Rules: 60–110 words (25–45 s). Numbers as words. At most 4 bracket tags. No "subscribe" begging, no "you won't believe". The hook and payoff must be true without context.
