# Style bible: The Invisible Explained

Recovered on 2026-11-04 from the channel's finished Shorts in Higgsfield (Oct 26 and 28: black hole, phone chip, Moon gone, lightning) and its voiceover history. Keep this identity in every video, whatever the visual mode.

## Format
- 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC. Finals so far ran 29.6–31.0 s; target 25–45 s.
- One idea per video. Hook in the first 2 s, payoff line at the end, then the end card.

## Scene mix
- 10 scenes per video: 4 photoreal (hook, the key mechanism, the contrast, the evidence) and 6 cartoon scenes drawn in code with the white mascot from the Oct 29 ice-cream tests (thick black outline, dot eyes, arched brows, noodle arms), on the navy backdrop.

## Look
- Palette: deep navy / near-black backgrounds (#05070D to #0B1220), cyan-teal accent (#3FD0D4 range), white type. Warm accents (amber/orange) only when the subject is hot (lightning, fire) or a "gate/state" readout.
- Subjects: one clear subject per scene, centred, high contrast against a dark field. Cinematic, documentary-grade lighting.
- Photoreal mode: real-looking anatomy, physics and places. Never gore, never fake "footage" of real events or real people.

## Type and overlays
- **Captions:** Montserrat ExtraBold, ALL CAPS, white, 2 lines max, centred in the lower third (about 70% of frame height). Word-by-word karaoke: spoken word turns cyan; 1–2 key terms per line stay cyan.
- **Scene label:** tiny monospace ALL-CAPS label at the top centre under a short cyan rule, e.g. `SILICON DIE · MAGNIFIED`, `GRAVITATIONAL LENSING`.
- **Honesty tags** in that label whenever a visual is not literal: `· ILLUSTRATIVE`, `· CONCEPTUAL`, `· PHYSICS STILL INCOMPLETE`, `· NOT TO SCALE`. This is part of the brand; keep it.
- **Data callouts:** big numerals for the key number (`30,000°C`, `0.25 s`, `23.4°`), with a small label above.
- **Opening frame** carries a small `THE INVISIBLE EXPLAINED` label; the **end card** is `THE INVISIBLE` (cyan) / `EXPLAINED` (white) on black, about 1.5 s.

## Voice
- Two fixed male narrators in one take (owner decision 2026-11-04, see `channel.json` → `voice`): **Alistair** (deep documentary) speaks over photoreal scenes, **Dylan** (young, playful) over cartoon scenes. No other voices.
- ElevenLabs v4, stability 0.40–0.45. One delivery tag at the start, at most 3 more inside: e.g. `[deep, cinematic documentary narrator, calm authority, steady pace]`, `[leaning in]`, `[building tension]`, `[pause]`.
- CAPITALS on one or two words for stress (`NEVER`, `BLOCKS`). Ellipses for beats.
- Numbers written as words in the voice script ("thirty thousand degrees"), as numerals on screen.

## Writing
- Open with a question or an instruction the viewer can feel ("Try tickling yourself.", "What would you ACTUALLY see if you fell into a black hole?").
- Second person, present tense, short sentences.
- Payoff line reframes the idea ("Coffee doesn't give your brain new energy. It temporarily changes how your brain senses fatigue.").
- Hedge exactly as much as the science does: "scientists think", "studies have found", "roughly". No "doctors hate this", no exaggerated numbers.

## Sound
- Voice at -14 LUFS integrated for the final mix, peaks below -1 dBTP.
- Low ambient bed and 2–4 subtle SFX (whoosh on cuts, a hit on the key number). Never loud over the voice.
