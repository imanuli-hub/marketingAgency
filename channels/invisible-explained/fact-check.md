# Fact-check standard

Every script passes this before any credits are spent. Record the result in the episode manifest (`factCheck`).

1. **List every claim** in the narration and on-screen text, one per row: numbers, mechanisms, "studies show", comparisons.
2. **Source each claim** with at least one primary or authoritative source: peer-reviewed paper, review article, university or government science body (NASA, NOAA, NIH, WHO), or a standard textbook. Popular articles may lead you to the source but are not the source.
3. **Match the hedging** to the evidence: consensus → plain statement; one study → "in one experiment"; contested → "scientists think".
4. **Numbers:** keep units, round honestly ("about", "over"), never inflate.
5. **Visual honesty:** if a scene is not literal (scale, colour, a model of a molecule), its scene label gets an honesty tag (see style bible).
6. **No medical advice.** Explain mechanisms; never tell viewers to do or stop anything for their health.
7. **Verdict:** `pass`, or `fail` with the claim to rewrite. A failed claim is rewritten or cut, never kept "because it sounds good".

Do not fabricate citations. If a source cannot be found, cut the claim.
